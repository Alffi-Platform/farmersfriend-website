Features

Everything you need to manage your farm efficiently

Irrigation

Smart Irrigation Scheduling

Take the guesswork out of irrigation with science-based scheduling that considers soil type, crop requirements, and weather conditions.

  • PAW-Based Scheduling Calculate irrigation needs based on Plant Available Water, soil texture, and root depth
  • Scheduling Wizard Step-by-step guide to create optimal irrigation schedules
  • Water Allocation Tracking Monitor usage against your annual water allocation with visual progress bars
  • Pump Engine Management Track engine hours, fuel consumption, and service intervals
  • Compliance Reporting Generate reports for water authority compliance with one tap
  • Calendar View Visualise your irrigation schedule in weekly calendar format
Field Operations

Plan & Execute Field Work

From spraying to spreading, plan your field operations with precision and track execution in real-time.

  • Spraying Operations Automatic mixing calculations, product tracking, and nozzle chart integration
  • Spreading Operations Calculate fertiliser requirements and track application rates
  • GPS Execution Tracking Record actual coverage with GPS path tracking and swath visualisation
  • A-B Line Guidance Set up parallel guidance lines for systematic field coverage
  • Product Estimates Real-time speed feedback and estimated product usage
  • Pre-Operation Checklists Ensure equipment is ready before starting operations
Tasks

Coordinate Your Team

Assign work, track progress, and collaborate with your team - whether they're in the paddock or the office.

  • Task Assignment Assign tasks to team members with due dates and priorities
  • Comments & Collaboration Add comments, photos, and @mention team members
  • Time Tracking Log hours worked on each task
  • Resource Requests Request materials, equipment, or assistance
  • Apple Reminders Integration Sync tasks with iOS Reminders for notifications
  • Link to Operations Connect tasks to field operations for complete traceability
Equipment

Manage Your Machinery

Keep track of all your farm equipment, from tractors to sprayers, with service reminders and mount tracking.

  • Machinery Register Track tractors, harvesters, and other machinery
  • Sprayer Management Configure boom width, tank capacity, and nozzle charts
  • Spreader Calibration Set up application rates and track calibration settings
  • Attachment Mounting Track what's mounted on each tractor with mount point management
  • Service Tracking Log service history and get reminders for upcoming maintenance
  • JSON Nozzle Import Import custom nozzle charts from manufacturers
Mapping

Know Your Property

Map your paddocks, mark important locations, and navigate around your property with confidence.

  • Paddock Boundaries Define area boundaries with markers that automatically calculate hectares
  • Infrastructure Markers Mark gates, tanks, pumps, and other infrastructure
  • Photo Markers Add geotagged photos to locations
  • GPS Navigation Navigate to any marker with turn-by-turn guidance
  • Irrigation Runs Map irrigation run paths from valve to target
  • Hybrid Maps Satellite imagery with labels for easy orientation
Cloud Sync

Work Together, Anywhere

Share data across your team's devices with automatic cloud synchronisation. Everyone stays up to date.

  • Real-Time Sync Changes sync automatically across all team devices
  • Offline Support Keep working without internet - syncs when you're back online
  • Enterprise Structure Organise by enterprise with multiple properties
  • Team Members Invite team members with role-based access control
  • Image Sync Photos attached to markers and tasks sync across devices
  • Secure Storage Data stored securely on Firebase with authentication
