Getting Started

1

Download the App

Download Farmers Friend from the App Store. The app requires iOS 18.0 or later and works on both iPhone and iPad.

2

Create Your Enterprise

Set up your enterprise (farm business) with a name. You can manage multiple properties under one enterprise.

3

Add Your Property

Add your first property with its location. The app will use this to centre maps and calculate weather data.

4

Map Your Paddocks

Use the Map tab to add markers around your property. Create Areas by grouping markers to define paddock boundaries.

5

Set Up Equipment

Add your machinery, sprayers, and spreaders in the Equipment section. Configure calibration settings for accurate calculations.

6

Invite Your Team

Go to Settings > Team Members to invite workers to your enterprise. They'll be able to view and update data on their devices.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sync data between devices?

Data syncs automatically when you're connected to the internet. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID on all devices and that cloud sync is enabled in Settings.

To manually trigger a sync, pull down on most list views to refresh.

Can I use the app offline?

Yes! Farmers Friend works fully offline. All your data is stored locally on your device. When you reconnect to the internet, changes will sync automatically to the cloud and other devices.

How do I calculate irrigation scheduling?

Go to Irrigation > Calendar or use the Scheduling Wizard. The app uses Plant Available Water (PAW) calculations based on your soil type, crop, and weather conditions to recommend optimal irrigation timing.

You'll need to set up irrigation runs first by marking valve and target locations on your map.

How do I add team members?

Go to Settings > Team Members > Add Member. Enter their email address and they'll receive an invitation. Once they download the app and sign in, they'll have access to your enterprise data.

Note: Adding team members requires a Professional or Enterprise subscription.

How do I set up spraying operations?

Go to Operations > Create Operation > Spraying. Select your target areas, choose your sprayer, add products with application rates, and the app will calculate the mixing requirements automatically.

Make sure your sprayer is configured with correct tank capacity and boom width in Equipment settings.

How do I import custom nozzle charts?

Go to Equipment > Sprayers > Select a sprayer > Nozzle Chart > Manage Chart. You can import a JSON file with your manufacturer's nozzle data, or use the built-in Teejet XR defaults.

The Format Info button shows the required JSON structure for custom imports.

How do I generate compliance reports?

Go to Irrigation > Reports > Compliance Report. Select the date range and the report will show all irrigation activities with water usage, suitable for water authority reporting.

You can export reports as PDF, CSV, or email them directly.

What devices are supported?

Farmers Friend requires iOS 18.0 or later. It works on iPhone and iPad. The app is optimised for iPhone but fully functional on iPad with a larger display.
