Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes! The Professional plan includes a 14-day free trial. No credit card required to start.

Can I change plans later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time through the App Store.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your data remains on your device. Cloud sync features will be disabled, but local data stays intact.

Do team members need their own subscription?

No. Your subscription covers all team members invited to your enterprise.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest using Firebase's secure infrastructure.

Can I use the app offline?

Yes. The app works fully offline and syncs when you have internet connectivity.
